About Riverline

Draftkings is a local, offline sports community organizing real, in-person tennis and basketball activities. We're not an online league or a booking marketplace — every session happens on a real court, with real people, at a real time.

How It Works

Browse upcoming tennis and basketball activities, open the one you want, and register with a few basic details. No account, password, or profile is ever required. Just show up with what's listed under "What to Bring."

Tennis Activities

Singles and doubles sessions across every skill level, from casual rallies to advanced clinics, held on hard and clay courts around the city.

Basketball Activities

5v5 matches, 3v3 tournaments, and open-gym sessions for all levels, held indoors at our partner sports halls.

Sports Locations

Participation Rules

Arrive 10 minutes before start time. Respect the skill level listed for each activity. Cancel as early as possible if you can't make it, so someone else can take the spot. Be respectful of courts, equipment, and fellow players.
